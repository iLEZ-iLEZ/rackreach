Box Fit XR

Two boxes at real scale. Grab them and move them around your room.

In the headset

Trigger or grip
Point at a box and hold to grab it. Boxes always stay upright.
Thumbstick
While holding: left/right turns the box, up/down pushes it away or pulls it closer.
A / X
Set the box you're pointing at (or holding) down on the floor.
B / Y
Bring both boxes back in front of you.
Label
Shows the size and how high the bottom of the box sits above the floor.
